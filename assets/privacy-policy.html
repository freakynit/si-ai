SI → AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SI → AI replaces “Super Intelligence” with “Artifical Intelligence” and whole-word “si” with “ai” on web pages.

The extension processes page text locally in your browser. It does not collect, store, or share personal information, browsing history, or page content.

Access to websites is used only to make these text replacements, including in dynamically loaded content.

The extension does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, remote code, or external servers.
